Jehoiada (M)

Name means “Knowledge of the Lord” — Hebrew

First named in 2 Samuel 8:18

OT Divided Monarchy

Jehoiada, high priest during the reign of the usurping queen Athaliah, was married to Jehosheba, who secretly rescued the infant Joash from Athaliah's massacre of the royal family and hid him in the temple (2 Kings 11:2-3). Jehoiada sheltered and raised the boy there for six years, and in the seventh year executed a carefully planned coup: he secured the loyalty of the temple guards and Levites, armed them with weapons from David's own armory, and presented the seven-year-old Joash publicly, anointing him king while Athaliah was seized and executed outside the temple (11:4-16). Jehoiada immediately led the people, the king, and himself in a covenant renewal to be "the LORD's people," then oversaw the demolition of Baal's temple in Jerusalem (11:17-18). Throughout Joash's youth, Jehoiada served as his guide and effectively as regent, and under his direction Joash ordered the temple, worn down and plundered during Athaliah's reign, to be repaired using funds collected from the people (2 Chronicles 24:4-14). Jehoiada lived to the remarkable age of 130 and was buried among the kings of Judah in the City of David, a rare honor for a priest, "because he had done good in Israel, both toward God and His house" (24:15-16). Tragically, Judah's faithfulness under Joash did not survive Jehoiada's death for long, as the king later turned to idolatry once his mentor was gone (24:17-18).

Jehoiada was a faithful high priest in Judah during a very dangerous time, when the wicked queen Athaliah had seized the throne and tried to wipe out the entire royal family. Jehoiada's wife secretly rescued the infant prince Joash and hid him in the temple, and Jehoiada protected and raised him there in secret for six years. When Joash turned seven, Jehoiada carefully organized guards and leaders to reveal Joash publicly and crown him as Judah's rightful king, ending Athaliah's reign. Jehoiada then guided young King Joash for many years afterward, leading the people to renew their covenant with God and repair the temple. Jehoiada lived to a very old age and was honored greatly at his death for all the good he had done for Judah.

Thought for Today

For six years Jehoiada hid the infant king Joash inside the temple, then staged a coup that overthrew Athaliah's usurped throne and restored God's chosen line to it. Jesus is the true King whose reign no violent usurper can ultimately erase, no matter how long His people wait to see it fully established.
