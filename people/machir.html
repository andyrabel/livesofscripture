Machir (M)

Name means “Selling; knowing” — Hebrew

First named in Genesis 50:23

OT Patriarchal

Machir was the son of Manasseh and grandson of Joseph, born in Egypt during the family's sojourn there -- Joseph "saw the third generation" through him, since "the children of Machir, the son of Manasseh, were born on Joseph's knees" (Genesis 50:23), a mark of the patriarch's long life and blessing. Machir's descendants, called Machirites, grew into a substantial clan within the tribe of Manasseh. Generations later, during Israel's conquest of the territory east of the Jordan, Scripture credits "the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh" with taking Gilead from the Amorites who lived there, after which "Moses gave Gilead to Machir the son of Manasseh, and he lived in it" (Numbers 32:39-40). Joshua 17:1 states the rationale directly: "Machir was the father of Gilead... because he was a man of war, therefore he had Gilead and Bashan." Since the conquest occurred centuries after Joseph's era, most readers understand these later references to Machir as describing his descendants acting and inheriting under his name -- a common pattern in Israel's tribal genealogies, where an ancestor's name stands for the clan he founded. Whether read of the individual or his line, the consistent thread is military strength winning and holding contested territory, and Machir's name became permanently attached to Gilead, a region so identified with his family that "Gilead" and "Machir" function almost interchangeably in later texts (e.g. Judges 5:14).

Machir was the son of Manasseh and grandson of Joseph. He was born in Egypt, and Joseph lived long enough to see Machir's own children. Machir's family, the Machirites, became strong and were known as skilled fighters. Later, when Israel was conquering land east of the Jordan River, Machir's descendants took over the regions of Gilead and Bashan and settled there. The Bible says this land was given to them because Machir was "a man of war" -- his family's courage and strength shaped where an entire branch of the tribe of Manasseh made their home for generations to come.

Thought for Today

Machir's descendants inherited Gilead because he was 'a man of war.' We inherit an eternal home in Jesus not by strength, but by His finished work.
