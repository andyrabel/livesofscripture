Hobab (M)

Name means “Favored; beloved” — Hebrew

First named in Numbers 10:29

OT Exodus/Wilderness

Hobab appears in Numbers 10 as Israel breaks camp at Sinai to begin the journey toward the promised land. He is identified as "the son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses' father-in-law" -- a phrase Scripture itself leaves ambiguous, since elsewhere Hobab, not Reuel, is called Moses' father-in-law (Judges 4:11), leading to long-standing uncertainty over whether Hobab and Jethro/Reuel are the same man under different names or two different relatives. Moses asked Hobab to travel with Israel: "We are setting out to the place... Come with us and we will do you good, for the LORD has promised good concerning Israel" (10:29). Hobab initially refused, wanting to return to his own land and relatives (10:30). Moses pressed him again, appealing to his practical value -- "you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you will be as eyes for us" -- and promised to share with him whatever good the LORD did for Israel (10:31-32). Numbers does not record Hobab's final decision, but later texts describing Kenite descendants of Moses' father-in-law living among Israel (Judges 1:16; 4:11) suggest that he, or at least his family, did go along. Whatever the outcome, the exchange shows Israel depending on ordinary human knowledge and relationships even while following God's own pillar of cloud and fire.

Hobab was a relative of Moses through marriage, from the Midianite family of Moses' father-in-law. As Israel prepared to leave Mount Sinai and travel through the wilderness, Moses asked Hobab to come along and help guide the way, since Hobab knew the desert well. At first Hobab said no -- he wanted to go back home to his own people and land. But Moses asked again, telling him how much they needed his help: "You will be our eyes," Moses said, promising that Israel would share with him whatever good God gave them. The Bible does not tell us for certain what Hobab decided, but later passages suggest his family did end up traveling and settling alongside Israel.

Thought for Today

Moses valued Hobab's wisdom even while Israel followed God's pillar of cloud and fire. God still guides us through His Spirit and the people He places around us.
