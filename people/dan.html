Dan (M)

First named in Genesis 30:6

OT Patriarchal

Dan was Jacob's fifth son, born to Bilhah, the maidservant Rachel had given to Jacob so that she might "obtain children" through her, since Rachel herself remained barren while her sister Leah bore sons freely (Genesis 30:1-3). When Bilhah's son was born, Rachel declared, "God has vindicated me, and has indeed heard my voice and has given me a son." Therefore she named him Dan" -- a name built on the Hebrew word for judging or vindicating (30:6). Dan is listed among the twelve sons who traveled to Egypt with Jacob (46:23) and among the tribal heads counted and positioned throughout Israel's wilderness years (Numbers 1-2). On his deathbed, Jacob gave Dan a distinct, somewhat ominous blessing: "Dan shall judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel. Dan shall be a serpent in the way, a horned snake in the path, that bites the horse's heels so that his rider falls backward" (Genesis 49:16-17) -- language suggesting cunning, ambush-style strength rather than open, conventional warfare. Scripture records no personal deeds or words of Dan himself beyond his birth and this blessing; his individual story is brief, but his name became permanent as one of the twelve tribes of Israel, later allotted territory in Canaan (Joshua 19:40-48) before much of the tribe migrated north (Judges 18).

Dan was one of the twelve sons of Jacob, born to Bilhah, the servant of Jacob's wife Rachel. Rachel had been unable to have children of her own for a long time, so when Bilhah's baby was born, Rachel felt like God had finally stood up for her, and she named the baby Dan, which means something like "he judged" or "vindication." Dan grew up to become the father of one of the twelve tribes of Israel. Near the end of his life, Jacob gave each of his sons a special blessing, and his words about Dan were short but memorable, comparing Dan's tribe to a clever snake.

Thought for Today

Rachel named her son Dan, believing God had finally vindicated her after years of waiting. God still vindicates those who wait on Him, most fully through Jesus.
