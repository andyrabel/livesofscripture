Shelumiel (M)

Name means “God is my peace” — Hebrew

First named in Numbers 1:6

OT Exodus/Wilderness

Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai was appointed leader of the tribe of Simeon at Mount Sinai, one of twelve tribal representatives named to assist Moses and Aaron in numbering Israel's fighting men (Numbers 1:5-6). The census counted 59,300 men of Simeon under his tribe (1:23), and in the camp's arrangement around the tabernacle, Shelumiel's division was set on the south side alongside Reuben and Gad (2:12-13). When the tabernacle was consecrated, each of the twelve leaders brought an identical, generously detailed dedication offering on his own appointed day; Shelumiel's fell on the fifth day, and he presented a silver dish and bowl filled with grain offering, a gold pan of incense, and animals for burnt, sin, and peace offerings -- two oxen, five rams, five male goats, and five male lambs (Numbers 7:36-41). When Israel broke camp to march, Shelumiel led Simeon's division out under Reuben's larger standard (10:18-19). Nothing sets Shelumiel apart individually beyond these repeated, formulaic duties -- he is one name among twelve who shared identical responsibilities -- but Scripture records his specific offering and role by name, marking him as a real, named leader trusted with real responsibility over one of Israel's twelve tribes during the wilderness years.

Shelumiel was chosen as the leader of the tribe of Simeon while Israel camped at Mount Sinai. God told Moses to appoint one leader from each of the twelve tribes to help count all the fighting men in Israel, and Shelumiel was Simeon's leader for this important job. When the new tabernacle was set up and dedicated to God, each of the twelve tribal leaders brought a generous gift on his own special day -- silver dishes, gold, oxen, and animals for offerings. Shelumiel brought his tribe's gift on the fifth day. He also led the tribe of Simeon whenever the whole camp packed up and marched forward through the wilderness.

References: Numbers 1:6; Numbers 2:12; Numbers 7:36-41; Numbers 10:19
