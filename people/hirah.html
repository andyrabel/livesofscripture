Hirah (M)

Name means “Liberty; anger” — Hebrew

First named in Genesis 38:1-20

OT Patriarchal

Hirah was a man of Adullam and a close friend of Judah, one of Jacob's twelve sons, during the years Judah had settled away from his father's household and married a Canaanite wife (Genesis 38:1-2). Hirah traveled with Judah to Timnah for a sheep-shearing feast after Judah's own wife died (38:12). It was on this trip that Judah, not recognizing his own daughter-in-law Tamar in disguise, propositioned her along the road and left his seal, cord, and staff as a pledge for payment he promised to send later (38:13-18). When Judah sent that payment -- a young goat -- he sent it through Hirah, asking him to retrieve the pledge items (38:20). Hirah searched at Enaim for the woman but could not find her; the local men told him no one of that description had been there, and Hirah reported this back to Judah, who let the matter drop rather than risk public shame (38:21-23). Tamar's later pregnancy exposed Judah's own sin when she produced his pledge as proof, and Judah confessed, "She is more righteous than I" (38:26). Hirah's role is small and functional -- errand-runner and discreet friend -- but Genesis names him twice by name and hometown, marking him as a real, remembered figure inside one of Scripture's most unflinching family narratives, the ancestry of Perez, forefather of David and, ultimately, Christ.

Hirah was a man from the town of Adullam who was a close friend of Judah, one of Jacob's sons. Hirah lived nearby and joined Judah for a big sheep-shearing celebration. Later, when Judah needed to send a payment to keep a promise he had made, he asked Hirah to deliver it and settle the matter for him. Hirah looked for the person Judah described but could not find her, and reported back that no one there knew who Judah meant. Hirah's part in the story is small, but he shows up as a loyal friend who helped Judah handle an awkward situation quietly, inside a bigger family story that God was still at work in.

Thought for Today

Hirah played a small part in the story of Judah and Tamar that led to Perez, an ancestor of Jesus. God works His plan through ordinary, overlooked people.
