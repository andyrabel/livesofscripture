Medad (M)

Name means “He that measures; water of love” — Hebrew

First named in Numbers 11:26-27

OT Exodus/Wilderness

Medad was one of seventy elders of Israel gathered by Moses at the LORD's command to help bear the burden of leading the people through the wilderness (Numbers 11:16-17). The LORD took of the Spirit that was on Moses and put it on the seventy elders gathered around the tent of meeting, and they prophesied (11:24-25). Two of the chosen elders, Eldad and Medad, had remained in the camp rather than going out to the tent -- yet 'the Spirit rested upon them... and they prophesied in the camp' anyway (11:26). A young man ran to report this to Moses, and Joshua, Moses's aide, urged him, 'Moses, my lord, restrain them' (11:27-28), apparently concerned that prophecy occurring outside the sanctioned gathering threatened Moses's authority. Moses's response has become one of the most quoted lines in the wilderness narrative: 'Are you jealous for my sake? Would that all the LORD's people were prophets, that the LORD would put His Spirit upon them!' (11:29). Medad's brief appearance illustrates that the Spirit's work is not confined to expected places or sanctioned gatherings, and Moses's reaction models humble leadership that welcomes rather than restricts the Spirit's activity in others.

When Moses had too much work leading the Israelites by himself, God told him to choose seventy elders to help share the responsibility. Most of the seventy went out to a special tent to receive God's Spirit, but two men, Eldad and Medad, stayed back in the camp. Surprisingly, God's Spirit came on them there too, and they began to prophesy right in the camp. A young man ran to tell Moses, and Joshua, worried about it, asked Moses to stop them. But Moses wasn't jealous at all. He said, 'I wish that all the LORD's people were prophets, that the LORD would put His Spirit on them!' This showed Moses's humble heart and his desire for everyone to know and follow God, not just himself.

Thought for Today

Medad prophesied in the camp, and Moses wished all the LORD's people had that same Spirit. Jesus later poured out that Spirit on all who believe in Him.
