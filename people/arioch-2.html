Arioch (M)

First named in Daniel 2:14-25

OT Exile

Arioch was captain of King Nebuchadnezzar's bodyguard in Babylon, tasked with carrying out the king's furious order to execute all of Babylon's wise men after none of them could tell Nebuchadnezzar the content of a troubling dream he refused to describe, let alone interpret it (Daniel 2:12-13). When Arioch came to seize Daniel and his three companions along with the rest, Daniel "replied with prudence and discretion" to him, asking directly why the king's decree was so urgent, and Arioch explained the situation (2:14-15). Rather than executing Daniel on the spot, Arioch allowed him to go before the king and request time, which Nebuchadnezzar granted (2:16). God then revealed both the dream and its interpretation to Daniel in a night vision (2:17-23). Daniel went back to Arioch, who had been ordered to destroy Babylon's wise men, and told him, "Do not destroy the wise men of Babylon; bring me into the king's presence, and I will declare the interpretation to the king" (2:24). Arioch hurried Daniel in before Nebuchadnezzar, taking credit for having "found a man" among the exiles who could make the interpretation known (2:25). Daniel then gave God, not himself, full credit for revealing the mystery (2:27-30), an interpretation that led Nebuchadnezzar to fall on his face and honor Daniel's God as "God of gods and Lord of kings" (2:46-47).

Arioch was the captain in charge of the king's guards in Babylon. King Nebuchadnezzar had a troubling dream and demanded his wise men tell him both the dream and its meaning, an impossible task, so he ordered Arioch to have all of Babylon's wise men put to death, including Daniel. But Daniel spoke to Arioch calmly and wisely, asking for time to seek an answer from God. Arioch agreed and brought Daniel before the king. God then showed Daniel both the dream and its meaning during the night, and many lives, including the wise men Arioch had been ordered to kill, were saved because of it.

Thought for Today

Arioch's death order was delayed for Daniel, and God revealed the king's dream that night. Jesus is the mystery of God made known to those who seek Him.
