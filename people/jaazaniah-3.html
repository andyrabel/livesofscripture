First named in Ezekiel 11:1

Jaazaniah (M)

OT Exile

Jaazaniah, son of Azzur, appears alongside Pelatiah son of Benaiah as one of twenty-five leading men Ezekiel saw in his temple vision, standing at the east gate of the LORD's house (Ezekiel 11:1). God identified them to Ezekiel as "the men who devise iniquity and give wicked counsel in this city," men who told the frightened remnant in Jerusalem, in effect, that the city was a safe pot and they the meat within it, secure from the judgment already falling on those exiled (11:2-3). Ezekiel was commanded to prophesy against them, pronouncing that the sword outside the city, not the walls themselves, would determine their end, and that they would be judged at the border of Israel (11:5-11). As Ezekiel prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah -- one of the twenty-five standing alongside Jaazaniah -- suddenly died, and Ezekiel fell on his face crying out in fear for the rest of the remnant (11:13). Jaazaniah himself is not recorded as dying in the vision, but his inclusion by name among these condemned counselors marks him as one Ezekiel singles out for giving Jerusalem's leadership false confidence at the very moment true repentance was needed.

In another part of the same vision God gave Ezekiel, the prophet was shown twenty-five leaders of Jerusalem standing at one of the temple gates. God told Ezekiel that these men were giving the city bad advice, the kind that would lead people further into trouble instead of turning back to God. One of them is named for us: Jaazaniah, son of Azzur. God had Ezekiel speak a hard message of judgment right there against these leaders, warning that their scheming would not save the city.
