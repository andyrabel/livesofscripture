Noah (F)

First named in Numbers 26:33

OT Exodus/Wilderness

Noah was one of five daughters of Zelophehad, a man of Manasseh's tribe who died in the wilderness leaving no sons (Numbers 26:33; 27:1) -- a different person from the flood account's Noah, sharing only an English spelling of an unrelated Hebrew name. Along with her sisters Mahlah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah, Noah came before Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the leaders of the whole congregation at the tent of meeting with a direct legal claim: their father had died for his own sin, not as part of Korah's rebellion, and it was unjust that his name should be lost from his clan simply because he had no son -- they asked for a possession among their father's brothers (27:2-4). Moses, uncertain how to rule, brought their case directly to the LORD, who answered unequivocally: 'The daughters of Zelophehad are right in their statements... you shall surely give them a hereditary possession' -- and God used the occasion to establish a permanent statute of inheritance law for Israel covering exactly this situation (27:5-11). Later, when the leaders of their tribe raised a follow-up concern about land passing out of the tribe through marriage, God's law was refined so the sisters were required to marry within their own tribe, which they did, marrying their cousins (36:1-12), and their inheritance was confirmed in the land allotment under Joshua (Joshua 17:3-6).

Noah was one of five sisters -- Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah -- whose father Zelophehad died in the wilderness with no sons to inherit his share of the promised land. Even though she shares a name with Noah who built the ark, this Noah was a different person entirely. The sisters bravely came before Moses and the leaders of Israel and asked for their father's inheritance to be given to them instead, since it wasn't fair for his name and land to disappear just because he had no sons. Moses brought their case to God, and God agreed with them completely, creating a new law so that daughters could inherit when there were no sons.

Thought for Today

Through Noah’s experience of inheritance, God teaches us to trust His wisdom and follow Jesus faithfully.

References: Numbers 26:33; Numbers 27:1-11; Numbers 36:1-12; Joshua 17:3-6
