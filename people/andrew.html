Andrew (M)

Name means “A strong man” — Greek

First named in Matthew 4:18

NT Gospels

Andrew, a fisherman from Bethsaida, was originally a disciple of John the Baptist. When John identified Jesus as "the Lamb of God," Andrew followed Jesus and spent the day with Him -- then immediately found his brother Simon and told him, "We have found the Messiah," bringing him to Jesus (John 1:35-42). Later, by the Sea of Galilee, Jesus called both brothers directly from their nets to "follow Me, and I will make you fishers of men," and they left everything at once (Matthew 4:18-20; Mark 1:16-18). Andrew was numbered among the Twelve (Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14) and was present with Peter, James, and John when they privately asked Jesus about the signs of the end times (Mark 13:3-4). His characteristic role in the Gospels is connecting people to Jesus: before the feeding of the five thousand, Andrew points out a boy with five barley loaves and two fish, though he doubts it can feed the crowd (John 6:8-9); when Greeks at the Passover feast asked to see Jesus, they approached Philip, who told Andrew, and together the two brought the request to Jesus (John 12:20-22). Andrew is last named in Scripture among the disciples gathered in prayer in Jerusalem after the ascension, awaiting the promised Holy Spirit (Acts 1:13-14). Church tradition holds he was later martyred, though Scripture does not record his death.

Andrew was a fisherman who first followed John the Baptist. When John pointed to Jesus and called Him "the Lamb of God," Andrew left to follow Jesus instead -- and the very first thing he did was go find his brother Simon Peter and bring him to meet Jesus too! Jesus later called both brothers to leave their fishing nets and follow Him permanently, making them two of His twelve apostles. Andrew shows up again and again doing the same kind of thing: bringing people to Jesus. He pointed out a boy with five loaves and two fish before Jesus fed a huge crowd, and he helped some visiting Greeks find their way to meet Jesus.

Thought for Today

The moment Andrew met Jesus, he ran to find his brother Simon and said, "We have found the Messiah" -- his very first instinct was to bring someone else to Him. Jesus still works that way, through ordinary people who can't help telling someone the moment they've found Him.

References: Matthew 4:18; Matthew 10:2; Mark 1:16-29; Mark 3:18; Mark 13:3; Luke 6:14; John 1:40-44; John 6:8; John 12:22; Acts 1:13
