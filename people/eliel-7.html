Eliel (M)

First named in 1 Chronicles 15:9-11

OT United Monarchy

Eliel led the clan of Hebron, one of the Kohathite Levite families, with eighty kinsmen under him in David's reorganization of Levitical service (1 Chronicles 15:9). He appears at a turning point in Israel's worship: David's first attempt to bring the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem had ended in disaster when Uzzah, steadying the ark on a cart, touched it and was struck dead, because it had not been carried the way the Law prescribed (1 Chronicles 13:7-10). Chastened, David gathered the priests and Levites and called Eliel by name along with the other Levitical chiefs -- Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, and Amminadab -- telling them, "You are the heads of the fathers' households of the Levites; consecrate yourselves, both you and your relatives, that you may bring up the ark of the LORD... to the place that I have prepared for it" (15:11-12). David added bluntly that their earlier failure came "because we did not seek Him according to the ordinance" (15:13). This time Eliel and his fellow Levites did it correctly: "the sons of the Levites carried the ark of God on their shoulders with the poles thereon, as Moses had commanded according to the word of the LORD" (15:15). The ark entered Jerusalem safely, and celebration followed. Eliel's part is brief but real: named obedience that corrected a costly earlier mistake, restoring right worship of the LORD in Israel's new capital.

Eliel was a Levite leader who served under King David. Earlier, David's men had tried to move the ark of the covenant -- the sacred box representing God's presence -- on a cart, but that wasn't how God said to carry it, and something went badly wrong along the way. David wanted to do it right the second time. He called Eliel and the other Levite leaders together and told them to make themselves clean and ready to serve God. Then Eliel and the others carried the ark on their shoulders using long poles, just the way God had commanded through Moses many years before. Because they listened and obeyed, the ark arrived safely in Jerusalem, and the whole city celebrated with joy.

Thought for Today

Eliel and the Levites finally carried the ark exactly as God commanded, correcting an earlier mistake. Jesus is the only way God appointed for us to approach Him.
