Joseph (M)

First named in Matthew 1:16-24

NT Gospels

Joseph was a Nazarene carpenter, legally descended from David (Matthew 1:16-20; Luke 3:23), betrothed to Mary. When he discovered she was pregnant before they had come together, he resolved to end the engagement quietly rather than expose her to public disgrace -- an honorable response given what he knew (Matthew 1:19). An angel then appeared to him in a dream, explaining the child was conceived by the Holy Spirit and instructing him to name the baby Jesus, "for He will save His people from their sins" (1:20-21). Joseph obeyed immediately, took Mary as his wife, and "kept her a virgin until she gave birth" (1:24-25). He traveled with her to Bethlehem for the census, where Jesus was born and laid in a manger, and Joseph was present when shepherds came to see the child (Luke 2:4-16). After the magi's visit, another dream warned him of Herod's murderous intent; Joseph fled with his family to Egypt by night, then later returned and settled in Nazareth once Herod was dead (Matthew 2:13-21). Scripture records no words spoken by Joseph -- only immediate, repeated obedience to divine instruction under real personal cost and danger. By the time of Jesus' public ministry Joseph is presumed dead, and neighbors still identified Jesus by his trade -- "Is not this the carpenter's son?" (John 6:42).

Joseph was a hardworking carpenter engaged to marry a young woman named Mary. When he learned Mary was going to have a baby before their wedding, he didn't understand what was happening -- but an angel appeared to him in a dream and told him not to be afraid, because the baby was from God and should be named Jesus. Joseph obeyed right away and married Mary. He traveled with her to Bethlehem, where Jesus was born, and was there when shepherds came to see the baby. Later, another dream warned Joseph that a dangerous king wanted to hurt Jesus, so Joseph quickly took his family to safety in Egypt. Joseph doesn't say much in the Bible, but every time God gave him instructions, he obeyed right away.

References: Matthew 1:16-24; Matthew 2:13-21; Luke 1:27; Luke 2:4-16; Luke 3:23; Luke 4:22; John 1:45; John 6:42
