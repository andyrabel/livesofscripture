Joah (M)

Name means “Fraternity; brother of the Lord” — Hebrew

First named in 1 Chronicles 6:21

OT Divided Monarchy

Joah, son of Zimmah, belonged to the Gershonite branch of the Levites (1 Chronicles 6:20-21). When Hezekiah became king of Judah and set about undoing the religious neglect of his father Ahaz's reign, he opened the doors of the temple and summoned the Levites, telling them, "consecrate yourselves now, and consecrate the house of the LORD... and carry the uncleanness out from the holy place" (2 Chronicles 29:3-5). Joah answered that call, named among the Levites "of the Gershonites" who arose alongside representatives from the Kohathite and Merarite clans (29:12) -- and notably, his own son Eden served in the same effort (29:12). Together these Levites gathered their brothers, consecrated themselves, and went to work removing everything unclean from the temple, carrying it out to the Kidron Valley over the course of eight days before the sanctuary itself could be reconsecrated and sacrifice resumed (29:15-19). Joah's name survives only within this list of participants, but the list itself records a real, dated act of obedience -- a family of Levites, father and son together, taking part in one of Judah's great moments of national repentance and renewal under a king determined to turn the nation back to the LORD.

When young King Hezekiah became king of Judah, he wanted to fix what earlier kings had broken -- the temple in Jerusalem had been neglected and filled with things that did not belong there. Hezekiah called the Levites together and told them to make themselves clean and get to work restoring God's house. Joah, from the Levite family of Gershon, was one of those who answered the call, and his own son Eden served right alongside him. Together with other Levite families, they went in and cleaned out the temple so that proper worship of the LORD could start again.

Thought for Today

Joah and his son together helped purify the temple that years of neglect had defiled. Jesus purifies us the same way, cleansing what neglect and sin have defiled.

References: 1 Chronicles 6:21; 2 Chronicles 29:12
