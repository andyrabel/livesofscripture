Noah (M)

First named in Genesis 5:29-32

OT Primeval History

Noah was the son of Lamech and a descendant of Seth, living in a generation Scripture describes as thoroughly corrupt: "the earth was filled with violence" and "every intent of the thoughts of man's heart was only evil continually" (Genesis 6:5,11). Amid this, Noah "found favor in the eyes of the LORD" and "walked with God" (6:8-9). God warned Noah of a coming flood to judge the earth and commanded him to build an ark of specific dimensions, then to bring his wife, three sons, their wives, and pairs of every living creature aboard. Noah obeyed "according to all that God had commanded him" (6:22), building the ark while, per 2 Peter 2:5's title for him, "a preacher of righteousness," the world took no heed. The flood came; every other living thing outside the ark perished, and after the waters receded, Noah's family and the animals emerged onto dry ground (Genesis 7-8). Noah built an altar and offered sacrifice, and God established a covenant, promising never again to destroy all flesh by flood, with the rainbow as its sign (9:1-17). Scripture does not spare Noah's own flaws: soon after, he became drunk on wine from his own vineyard and lay exposed in his tent, leading to his son Ham's disgraceful conduct and Noah's resulting curse on Canaan (9:20-27) -- even the flood's survivor was no sinless man.

Long ago, the world became so full of violence and sin that God decided to send a flood to wash it clean and start over. But one man, Noah, still walked with God and pleased Him. God told Noah to build a huge boat called an ark and gather his family and two of every kind of animal. Noah obeyed, even though it probably seemed strange to his neighbors. Rain fell for forty days, and the flood covered the earth, but Noah's family and the animals stayed safe inside the ark. When the waters finally went down, Noah stepped out onto dry land, built an altar, and thanked God. God then made a promise, sealed with a rainbow: He would never flood the whole earth again.

Thought for Today

God judged the world but saved Noah's family through the ark. Jesus, You are my ark — the only safe place from judgment.
