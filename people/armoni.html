Armoni (M)

First named in 2 Samuel 21:8

OT United Monarchy

Armoni was one of two sons born to Saul by his concubine Rizpah (2 Samuel 21:8). During David's reign, a three-year famine struck Israel, and David learned it was because of "Saul and his bloody house, because he put the Gibeonites to death," violating an oath Israel's leaders had sworn generations earlier under Joshua (21:1-2; see Joshua 9). Seeking to make atonement, David asked the Gibeonites what they wanted, and they demanded seven of Saul's male descendants be handed over for execution. David gave them Armoni and his brother Mephibosheth (not the same Mephibosheth as Jonathan's crippled son, whom David spared for Jonathan's sake), along with five sons of Saul's daughter Merab, and the Gibeonites hanged all seven "before the LORD" at Gibeah (21:6-9). Rizpah, Armoni's mother, then took sackcloth and kept vigil over the exposed bodies from the beginning of the harvest until the rains finally came, driving off birds and animals by day and night (21:10). When David heard of her devotion, he retrieved the bones of Saul and Jonathan along with the bones of the seven and gave all of them proper burial in the family tomb, "and after that God was moved by prayer for the land" (21:12-14). Armoni's death is one of Scripture's starkest reminders that sin's consequences can outlast the sinner and fall on those who never chose it -- and that even in the middle of that grief, faithful love like Rizpah's is seen and answered by God.

Armoni was one of King Saul's sons. Years after Saul died, a severe famine struck the land, and God told King David it was because Saul had broken an old promise to protect a group of people called the Gibeonites and had killed many of them instead. To make things right, David handed over Armoni, his brother, and five other relatives of Saul to the Gibeonites, and they were put to death. It's a hard and sad story. Afterward, Armoni's mother, Rizpah, showed remarkable love and courage, guarding her son's body from wild animals and birds for months until David finally gave all of them a proper, honorable burial.

Thought for Today

Armoni's death paid for a bloodguilt his father Saul had caused, an innocent bearing another's sin. Jesus, truly innocent, bore our sin so we would never have to.
