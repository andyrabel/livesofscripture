Jehoahaz (M)

Also called: Joahaz

First named in 2 Kings 10:35

OT Divided Monarchy

Jehoahaz became king of Israel after his father Jehu died, reigning seventeen years from Samaria (2 Kings 13:1). Like every king of the northern kingdom before him, "he did evil in the sight of the LORD, and followed the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat... he did not turn from them" (13:2). Because of this persistent idolatry, God's anger burned against Israel, and He repeatedly handed the nation over to Hazael king of Aram and his son Ben-hadad, who oppressed Israel throughout Jehoahaz's reign (13:3). In the midst of that oppression, Jehoahaz did something notable: he "entreated the favor of the LORD," and the LORD listened, because He saw how severely the king of Aram was crushing Israel (13:4). God responded by raising up an unnamed deliverer who rescued Israel from Aram's grip, allowing the people to live in their homes again as before (13:5). Yet the reprieve produced no lasting change of heart -- the people continued in the sins of Jeroboam's house, and the Asherah pole remained standing in Samaria (13:6). By the time Jehoahaz died, Aram's earlier campaigns had ground his army down to almost nothing: fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers (13:7). He was buried in Samaria, and his son Joash succeeded him (13:8-9).

Jehoahaz became king of Israel after his father, Jehu. Like the kings before him, Jehoahaz let his people keep worshiping false gods instead of the true God, so the Lord allowed Israel's enemy, the king of Aram, to defeat them again and again. Israel's army became very small and weak. But Jehoahaz did one good thing: he asked the Lord for help. God heard him and sent Israel someone to rescue them from the Arameans. Sadly, even after being rescued, the people still did not stop their idol worship. Jehoahaz ruled for seventeen years before he died and his son Joash became king.

Thought for Today

Jehoahaz cried out to God under oppression, and God listened and sent deliverance. We don't need to be saved again -- we simply need God's grace, every day.

References: 2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:1-25; 2 Kings 14:1-17; 2 Chronicles 25:17-25
