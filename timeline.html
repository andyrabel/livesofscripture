Timeline

  • Hatched, faded-edge bars are era estimates with genuinely disputed dates; solid bars have firmer dating. Hover a bar for specifics.
  • Bar length isn't to a consistent scale — most are a generic placeholder, but where Scripture states a lifespan directly (e.g. Methuselah, 969 yrs), that number sets the bar's length.
  • Name-only entries (no narrative, just a genealogy mention) are hidden by default — use the checkbox above to include them.
