Timeline

Why do some bars look different? Hatched, faded-edge bars mark era-only placements for figures whose exact dates are genuinely disputed among scholars; solid bars mark figures with firmer, cross-referenced dating. Hover any bar for specifics.

Why are some era bars much longer than others? A person's left-right position within an era is always an estimate, but a few dozen figures (Methuselah, Noah, Abraham, Moses, and others) have a total lifespan Scripture states directly — "all his days were 969 years, and he died" (Genesis 5:27) — and for those people, that stated number sets the bar's length, not a generic placeholder. Hover a long bar to see the reference.

Name-only entries are people who appear in Scripture only in a genealogy or list, with no narrative of their own — hidden by default to keep the timeline readable, but available via the checkbox above for tracing full family lines.
