Assyria

First named: Genesis 10:11
Nation Mesopotamia Divided Monarchy

A powerful Mesopotamian empire that became the northern kingdom of Israel's chief threat, exacting tribute under kings like Tiglath-pileser and Shalmaneser before finally conquering and deporting Israel's population in 722 BC under Sargon (2 Kings 15:19-20; 17:1-6). Assyria under Sennacherib also besieged Jerusalem in Hezekiah's reign, only to withdraw after God struck down the Assyrian army (2 Kings 18-19). Jonah was sent to preach repentance to Assyria's capital, Nineveh (Jonah 1:1-2).

References: Genesis 10:11; 2 Kings 15:19-20; 2 Kings 17:1-6; 2 Kings 18-19
