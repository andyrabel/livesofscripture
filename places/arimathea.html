Arimathea

First named: Matthew 27:57
Town Canaan Israel Gospels

Hometown of Joseph, a wealthy member of the Jewish council and secret disciple of Jesus who asked Pilate for Jesus's body and buried it in his own new tomb (Matthew 27:57-60; John 19:38-42).
