Edom

Also called: Seir
First named: Genesis 36:1
Nation Canaan Israel PatriarchalUnited MonarchyDivided MonarchyExile

The nation descended from Esau (also called Edom), settled in the mountainous region of Seir south of the Dead Sea. Edom refused Israel passage during the wilderness years (Numbers 20:14-21) and remained a persistent, often bitter rival through the monarchy — rebelling against Judah's control under Jehoram (2 Kings 8:20-22) and later gloating over Jerusalem's fall, which the book of Obadiah condemns at length.

References: Genesis 36:1; Numbers 20:14-21; 2 Kings 8:20-22; Obadiah
