Antioch

Also called: Antioch of Syria
Modern location: Antakya, Turkey
First named: Acts 11:19
City Asia Minor Greece GospelsApostolic

A major Syrian city where believers scattered by persecution first preached the gospel to Gentiles in large numbers, and where the disciples were first called "Christians" (Acts 11:19-26). Barnabas brought Saul there to help teach the growing church, and it was from Antioch that the Holy Spirit set apart Barnabas and Saul for the first missionary journey (Acts 13:1-3) — making the city the base of operations for the gospel's spread into the Gentile world. Peter's hypocrisy over eating with Gentiles was also confronted by Paul there (Galatians 2:11-14).

References: Acts 11:19; Acts 11:19-26; Acts 13:1-3; Galatians 2:11-14
