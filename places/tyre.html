Tyre

Modern location: Tyre, Lebanon
First named: Joshua 19:29
City Asia Minor Greece United MonarchyApostolic

A wealthy Phoenician port city whose king Hiram supplied cedar, craftsmen, and materials to David and Solomon for their building projects, including the temple, in a long friendly alliance (2 Samuel 5:11; 1 Kings 5:1-12). Paul's ship stopped there and he spent a week with Tyre's disciples on his final journey to Jerusalem (Acts 21:3-6).

References: Joshua 19:29; 2 Samuel 5:11; 1 Kings 5:1-12; Acts 21:3-6
