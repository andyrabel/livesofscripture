Hill Country of Ephraim

Also called: Mount Ephraim
First named: Joshua 17:15
Region Canaan Israel Judges

The forested highland territory allotted to Ephraim, a base of operations for several judges: Ehud rallied Israel's army from there after killing Eglon (Judges 3:26-27), and Deborah held court under a palm tree in the same hill country (Judges 4:4-5).

References: Joshua 17:15; Judges 3:26-27; Judges 4:4-5; Judges 17:1
