Valley of Elah

First named: 1 Samuel 17:2
Valley Canaan Israel United Monarchy

The valley where the Israelite and Philistine armies camped on opposite hillsides while Goliath issued his daily challenge, and where the shepherd boy David killed him with a sling and stone (1 Samuel 17).

References: 1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:1-54

People connected to Valley of Elah

  • Abinadab (M) — David's brother, serving in Saul's army here during the standoff with Goliath

    1 Samuel 17:13

  • Eliab (M) — David's eldest brother, who rebuked him for coming to the battle line here

    1 Samuel 17:28

  • Goliath (M) — defied Israel's army in this valley for forty days until David killed him

    1 Samuel 17:2-4; 1 Samuel 17:49
