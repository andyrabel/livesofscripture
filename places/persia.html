Persia

Modern location: Iran
First named: 2 Chronicles 36:20
Nation Mesopotamia Post-Exile/Intertestamental

The empire that conquered Babylon and, under Cyrus, issued the decree allowing exiled Jews to return and rebuild Jerusalem's temple (Ezra 1:1-4; 2 Chronicles 36:20-23). Esther became queen of Persia under Ahasuerus, and Daniel served in its administration under Darius, surviving the lions' den (Daniel 6).

References: 2 Chronicles 36:20; Ezra 1:1-4; Esther 1:1; Daniel 6

People connected to Persia

  • Artaxerxes (M) — its king, who sent Ezra and later Nehemiah to Jerusalem

    Ezra 7:11-13; Nehemiah 2:1-8

  • Cyrus (M) — its king, who decreed the exiles' return to rebuild the temple

    Ezra 1:1-4

  • Darius (M) — its king, who confirmed Cyrus's decree and funded the temple's completion

    Ezra 6:1-12

  • Mithredath (M) — Cyrus's treasurer, who counted out the temple vessels for the return

    Ezra 1:8
