Midian

First named: Exodus 2:15
Region Midian Exodus/WildernessJudges

A region and people east of the Gulf of Aqaba, descended from Abraham through Keturah (Genesis 25:1-2). Moses fled to Midian after killing an Egyptian, married Jethro's daughter Zipporah, and received his call from the burning bush while shepherding there (Exodus 2-4). Midianite women later helped lead Israel into idolatry at Baal-peor (Numbers 25), and generations later Midianite raiders oppressed Israel until Gideon, with a vastly outnumbered force, routed them (Judges 6-8).

References: Exodus 2:15; Exodus 2-4; Numbers 25; Judges 6-8
