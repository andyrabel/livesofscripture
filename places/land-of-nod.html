Land of Nod

First named: Genesis 4:16
Region Mesopotamia Primeval History
Location uncertain: "Nod" (meaning "wandering") is not identified with any known location; it names Cain's condition as much as a place.

The place east of Eden where Cain settled after God banished him for murdering his brother Abel (Genesis 4:16).

The Land of Nod was the region east of Eden where Cain went to live after God sent him away for killing his brother Abel. Its name means "wandering."
