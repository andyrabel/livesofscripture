Makkedah

First named: Joshua 10:10
Town Canaan Israel Exodus/Wilderness
Location uncertain: Site not confidently identified with a modern location.

A town near which five Amorite kings, defeated in Joshua's southern campaign, hid in a cave and were captured, executed, and buried by Joshua (Joshua 10:16-28).

References: Joshua 10:10; Joshua 10:16-28
