Acts — Main Characters by Chapter

Acts famously pivots partway through from a Peter-centered church to a Paul-centered mission. Each row below is a person; each bar is a chapter, colored by whoever the text's own narrative centers on in that chapter — Peter's early ministry in Jerusalem, Stephen's speech and martyrdom, Philip's mission to Samaria and the Ethiopian official, and then Paul's conversion and missionary journeys, which dominate the second half of the book. Acts 15's Jerusalem Council, where several voices speak and no single figure drives the chapter, is grouped under Jerusalem Council. Barnabas gets his own row too, overlapping the chapters he shares with Peter, Paul, and the Council — he's a major secondary worker throughout, not the chapter's primary storyteller, so his bars sit alongside those rows' colors rather than replacing them. Bars are clickable and link to that person's page; hover or focus a bar for a chapter summary.

Ch. 9 splits between Saul's conversion (9:1-31, the larger and more significant portion) and Peter's healing of Aeneas and raising of Dorcas (9:32-43); the whole chapter is grouped under Paul. Saul is renamed Paul partway through the book (13:9); the row is labeled “Paul” throughout for consistency. Barnabas's own row spans Acts 9 (vouching for Saul in Jerusalem) and Acts 11-15 (the Antioch mission, the first missionary journey, and the Jerusalem Council, ending with his and Paul's split over John Mark) — chapter-granularity spans, so his bar doesn't imply he's foregrounded in every verse of that range (e.g. most of ch. 12 is Peter's escape from prison; Barnabas reappears only at 12:25).

7StephenActs 7Stephen's speech before the Sanhedrin retells Israel's history; he is stoned to death, the first Christian martyr.
8PhilipActs 8Persecution scatters the believers; Philip preaches in Samaria, confronts Simon the sorcerer, and leads an Ethiopian official to Christ.
9PaulActs 9Saul is converted on the road to Damascus and begins preaching Christ (9:1-31); the chapter closes with Peter healing Aeneas and raising Dorcas (9:32-43).
9Barnabas (also)Acts 9Barnabas vouches for the newly converted Saul before Jerusalem's suspicious believers, describing his encounter with the Lord and his bold preaching in Damascus.
10PeterActs 10Peter, guided by a vision, brings the gospel to Cornelius, a Gentile centurion, whose household receives the Holy Spirit.
11PeterActs 11Peter defends his visit to Cornelius before the Jerusalem church; the Antioch church is founded, and Barnabas brings Saul there to teach.
11–15Barnabas (also)Acts 11Sent to the new church at Antioch, Barnabas fetches Saul from Tarsus to teach there, carries relief funds to Judea, and is set apart with Saul (now Paul) for their first missionary journey; after the Jerusalem Council, the two part ways over whether to bring John Mark again.
12PeterActs 12Herod kills James, the brother of John, and imprisons Peter, who is freed by an angel; Herod is struck down and dies.
13PaulActs 13Barnabas and Saul are sent out from Antioch; on Cyprus, Saul (now called Paul) blinds the sorcerer Elymas and preaches in Pisidian Antioch.
14PaulActs 14Paul and Barnabas preach in Iconium and Lystra, where Paul heals a lame man, is stoned and left for dead, then continues on.
15Jerusalem CouncilActs 15The Jerusalem Council debates whether Gentile believers must be circumcised; Peter, Paul, Barnabas, and James all speak, and a decision is sent out.
16PaulActs 16Paul recruits Timothy, is called in a vision to Macedonia, converts Lydia at Philippi, and is imprisoned with Silas before an earthquake frees them.
17PaulActs 17Paul preaches in Thessalonica and Berea, then reasons with the philosophers at the Areopagus in Athens.
18PaulActs 18Paul ministers in Corinth alongside Priscilla and Aquila and is brought before the proconsul Gallio.
19PaulActs 19In Ephesus, Paul baptizes disciples of John, works miracles, and a riot breaks out among the silversmiths over Artemis worship.
20PaulActs 20Paul raises Eutychus, who fell asleep and dropped from a window, at Troas, then gives a farewell address to the Ephesian elders at Miletus.
21PaulActs 21Paul is warned by the prophet Agabus, arrives in Jerusalem, and is seized in the temple by an angry crowd.
22PaulActs 22Paul addresses the crowd from the barracks steps, recounting his conversion, until he claims his Roman citizenship.
23PaulActs 23Paul divides the Sanhedrin over the resurrection; warned of a plot to kill him, he is sent under guard to Felix in Caesarea.
24PaulActs 24Paul is accused before governor Felix and gives his defense; Felix leaves him in custody for two years, hoping for a bribe.
25PaulActs 25The new governor Festus hears Paul's case; Paul appeals to Caesar and is presented before King Agrippa.
26PaulActs 26Paul gives his defense before Agrippa, again recounting his conversion; Agrippa says he is “almost persuaded” to become a Christian.
27PaulActs 27Paul sails for Rome as a prisoner; the ship is caught in a storm and wrecked off the island of Malta.
28PaulActs 28On Malta, Paul survives a viper's bite and heals the sick; he arrives in Rome and preaches the kingdom of God under house arrest.
