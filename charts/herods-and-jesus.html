The Herods, John the Baptist & Jesus

Six rulers of the Herod family appear in the New Testament, spanning nearly a century and a half. This chart lays their reigns against the lifetimes of John the Baptist and Jesus, so it is easy to see which Herod stood where in the Gospel and Acts narratives. Bars are clickable and link to that person's page; hover or focus a bar for exact dates.

Herod ruler (reign) John the Baptist (life) Jesus (life)

New Testament dates rest on Roman-era cross-references and are far firmer than Old Testament chronology, but two points are genuinely disputed and marked “c.” throughout: the death of Herod the Great (most place it in 4 BC; some argue 1 BC) and the date of the crucifixion (c. AD 30, though a significant number of evangelical scholars hold AD 33). The chart follows the more common 4 BC / AD 30 framework; shifting to the alternative moves John's and Jesus's bars a few years later but changes none of the relationships the chart shows.
