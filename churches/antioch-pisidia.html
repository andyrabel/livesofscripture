Antioch (Pisidia)

NT Galatia (Roman province)

A synagogue congregation on Paul and Barnabas's first missionary journey where Paul's sermon drew wide interest, provoked jealousy from some of the synagogue leaders, and led the two to turn deliberately toward preaching to the Gentiles.

References: Acts 13:14-52
