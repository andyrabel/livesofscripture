Samaria

NT Samaria

The first church outside Judea proper, planted when persecution scattered believers from Jerusalem -- Philip's preaching and miracles drew large crowds, including a well-known sorcerer whose profession of faith Peter later called into serious doubt.

References: Acts 8:4-25

Named in Scripture at Samaria

  • Philip (M) — evangelist; preached Christ and performed signs that drew large crowds

    Acts 8:5-13

  • Simon (M) — sorcerer who professed belief and was baptized, then rebuked by Peter for trying to buy the Spirit's power with money

    Acts 8:9-24
