Lystra

NT Galatia (Roman province)

The city where a crowd first tried to worship Paul and Barnabas as gods, then stoned Paul and left him for dead, and where -- on Paul's return visit -- a young disciple named Timothy joined him as a missionary companion.

References: Acts 14:6-20; Acts 16:1-3; 2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 3:11
