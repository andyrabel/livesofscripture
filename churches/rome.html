Rome

NT Italy

A church Paul had not founded and had not yet visited when he wrote Romans, already large enough for him to send greetings to over two dozen named believers scattered across several house congregations; he later reached the city himself as a prisoner, and still had fellow believers with him during his second imprisonment there.

References: Acts 28:14-31; Romans 1:1-15; Romans 16
