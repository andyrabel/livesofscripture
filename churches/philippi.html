Philippi

NT Macedonia

The first church Paul planted in Europe, begun with a small group of women praying by a river. Paul and Silas were later beaten and jailed here, and the church that grew from this beginning became one of Paul's most affectionate and financially generous partners in the gospel.

References: Acts 16:11-40; Philippians 1:1
