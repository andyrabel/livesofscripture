Thessalonica

NT Macedonia

A church founded during a brief but fruitful three-week synagogue ministry, whose faith Paul says "rang out" well beyond Macedonia -- even though its founding provoked a mob and a formal complaint to the city officials.

References: Acts 17:1-9; 1 Thessalonians 1:1

    Acts 17:1-4; 1 Thessalonians 1:1

  • Silas (M) — founding missionary, co-sender of both letters to this church

    Acts 17:1-4; 1 Thessalonians 1:1

  • Timothy (M) — co-sender of both letters to this church; sent back to strengthen them

    1 Thessalonians 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:1-6

  • Jason (M) — host who was dragged before the city officials for sheltering Paul and Silas

    Acts 20:4

  • Demas (M) — deserted Paul's ministry, "having loved this present world," and went here
