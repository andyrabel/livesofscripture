Troas

NT Asia (Roman province)

A coastal city where believers gathered to break bread on the first day of the week -- the setting for Paul's all-night sermon during which a young man dozed off, fell from a third-story window, and was raised back to life.

References: Acts 20:5-12; 2 Corinthians 2:12; 2 Timothy 4:13

Named in Scripture at Troas

  • Paul (M) — preached through the night here on his final journey to Jerusalem

    Acts 20:6-12

  • Eutychus (M) — fell asleep during Paul's long sermon, fell from a window, and was raised up alive

    Acts 20:9-12
