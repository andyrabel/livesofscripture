Antioch (Syria)

NT Syria

The first church with a large Gentile membership, and the place believers were first called "Christians." It commissioned Paul and Barnabas for what became the first missionary journey, and was later the flashpoint for the debate over circumcising Gentile converts that the Jerusalem council settled -- including a sharp public rebuke Paul gave Peter here over table fellowship with Gentiles.

References: Acts 11:19-30; Acts 13:1-3; Acts 15:1-2; Acts 15:30-35
