Joppa

NT Judea

Home to Tabitha (Dorcas), a disciple whose death and raising by Peter led many in the city to believe, and to Simon the tanner, whose house Peter was staying in when he received the vision that opened the gospel to Gentiles.

References: Acts 9:36-43

Named in Scripture at Joppa

    Acts 9:36-42

  • Simon (M) — tanner who hosted Peter

    Acts 9:43; Acts 10:6-32
