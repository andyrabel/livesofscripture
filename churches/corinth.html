Corinth

NT Achaia

A church Paul planted during an eighteen-month stay, drawn from a cosmopolitan, morally loose port city, and one whose factions, lawsuits, and tolerance of gross sin gave Paul more to correct by letter than almost any other congregation -- while also including believers of real social standing, like its city treasurer.

References: Acts 18:1-18; 1 Corinthians 1:1-2

Named in Scripture at Corinth

  • Paul (M) — founding missionary, stayed eighteen months

    Acts 18:1-11

  • Silas (M) — present with Paul when he devoted himself fully to preaching here

    Acts 18:5

  • Aquila (M) — host and fellow tentmaker; later helped instruct Apollos more accurately

    Acts 18:1-3; Acts 18:26

  • Priscilla (F) — host and fellow tentmaker; later helped instruct Apollos more accurately

    Acts 18:1-3; Acts 18:26

    Acts 18:7

    Acts 18:17; 1 Corinthians 1:1

  • Apollos (M) — eloquent teacher who "watered" the church Paul had planted

    Acts 18:24-28; 1 Corinthians 3:6

  • Gaius (M) — hosted Paul and "the whole church"; one of the few Paul personally baptized

    1 Corinthians 1:14; Romans 16:23

  • Erastus (M) — the city treasurer

    Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20

  • Stephanas (M) — his household were the first converts in Achaia, "the firstfruits"; devoted themselves to serving the saints

    1 Corinthians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 16:15-17

  • Forunatus (M) — traveled to encourage Paul on the church's behalf

    1 Corinthians 16:17

    1 Corinthians 16:17

  • Chloe (F) — her household reported the church's divisions to Paul

    1 Corinthians 1:11

  • Timothy (M) — sent by Paul to remind the church of his ways in Christ

    1 Corinthians 4:17; 1 Corinthians 16:10
